Confirmation and step-up in Telegram

1. Approve a payment

Server-to-server confirmation: the backend asks a person to approve an action, with no sign-in and no redirect. Once confirmed, it learns who confirmed from an id_token.

Scan the code with the phone that has Telegram, press Start in the bot and answer.

2. Step-up before deleting a project

    
Deleting a project cannot be undone, so the backend asks the owner of the account to
      confirm and deletes only when the person who confirmed is that owner. The first person to
      confirm a deletion becomes the owner.
